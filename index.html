How pay is worked out
Rates come from the DP World Sydney Enterprise Agreement 2024 (VSE Operations): clause 11 hourly rates by grade, shift penalties (14.4), overtime and extensions (15.2, Part B 1.18.6), public holiday and closed port day rates (30.5), consolidated allowance per shift (18.7), meal allowance (11.6.2) and the VSE minimum salary (18.5).
Each shift is 8 paid hours. Night shifts are dated by the day they start, the same as your payslips. A Saturday-start night is ×2.5 and a Sunday-start night is ×2.
Extra hours (extensions or early starts) are ×2 Mon–Sat and ×2.5 Sunday. On a public holiday or closed port day, extra hours use the holiday rate. That last part is my best reading of the agreement, so check it against your payslip.
Six in a row: you can't work more than six shifts in a row, sick days included (Part B 1.11.1). After six, the next day is set to Off automatically. If you put a shift there yourself, it's kept and you get a warning.
Scratches: up to 27 scratched shifts per 28-day period (two pay fortnights). Go over 27 and the VSE minimum salary doesn't apply for that period (18.1.3, 18.3.1). Scratch days aren't paid.
A sick day (personal/carer's leave) pays 7 hours at $55.50 ($388.51), the same as your payslips show.
Meal allowance is added automatically where your payslips show it: shifts paid at the Sunday rate, shifts with an extension, and holidays worked. You can untick it on any shift.
Holidays (Part B 1.21.4): a closed port day covers its day, evening and the night starting that evening. A public holiday covers the night starting the evening before, then the day and evening. The night starting on the holiday evening is a normal shift.
Pay fortnights run Monday to Sunday, matching your payslips (for example 12–25 Oct 2026, paid Thu 29 Oct). If a fortnight's earnings are under the VSE minimum salary, you're paid the minimum and the gap is recovered from later pays (18.5.5).
Tax is worked out on gross less meal allowance and VSE PAID, at 2026–27 resident rates plus the 2% Medicare levy. Against your recent payslips it lands within $1–3. Super is 12% of gross, not counting VSE leave pay.
